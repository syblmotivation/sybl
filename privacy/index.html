Privacy Policy

What SYBL stores, why it stores it, who else ever sees it, and how to get rid of all of it.

Last updated September 3, 2026 · Applies to the SYBL mobile app for iOS and Android.

Who is responsible for your data

SYBL is operated by its developer, SYBL (“we”, “us”). For anything in this policy, including a request to access or delete your data, write to adictbart@gmail.com.

The short version

SYBL asks for an email address, a display name, your time zone and six onboarding answers. It then records the tasks it gives you and whether you completed them. That is all of it.

There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDK, no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app, in about ten seconds.

What we collect

Everything below is either typed in by you or produced by using the app. Nothing is gathered in the background.

Data Why it exists
Email address It identifies your account and is where the sign-up verification code is sent. You cannot use SYBL without one.
Password Stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Neither we nor anyone else can read it back.
Display name Chosen by you during onboarding and shown to you on your own profile screen. Nobody else ever sees it — SYBL has no social features, no profiles to browse and no leaderboards.
Time zone Read once from your device during onboarding, as an IANA identifier such as Europe/Zagreb. It decides when your day rolls over, which is what makes a streak meaningful. It is a region, not a location: it does not identify your city, and no GPS or network location is ever requested.
Onboarding answers Your selections for six multiple-choice questions: which area needs the most work, what else you are working on, how much time you have on a normal day, where you are starting from, what usually breaks your consistency, and when you are most likely to follow through. They are stored as references to the options you picked. There is no free-text box.
Focus areas Which of the six areas — Fitness, Mind, Discipline, Career, Social, Finance — you are working on, and in what order of importance. This is what personalises your daily tasks.
Daily tasks The four tasks generated for each of your days: title, description, area, difficulty, whether you completed it and when.
Progress Total XP, level, current and longest streak, perfect days, tasks completed, and a record of each XP award. All of it is calculated on our server, never sent up from your phone.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No location. The app never requests location permission and cannot read your position.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or health data. The Android build requests exactly one system permission — internet access — and the iOS build requests none.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers. No advertising ID, no IDFA, no fingerprinting, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no ad network in the app.
  • No analytics or behavioural SDK. There is no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel and no crash-reporting SDK in the app.
  • No payment information. SYBL takes no payments and has no subscriptions.
  • No health or medical data. SYBL is a habit and self-improvement app. It does not diagnose, treat or manage any medical or mental-health condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

How your data is used

Your data is used for exactly four things:

  1. To sign you in and keep you signed in between launches.
  2. To choose and word your daily tasks from your focus areas, your onboarding answers and what you have recently been given, so the same task is not repeated at you.
  3. To keep score — XP, level, streak and history — which is the point of the app.
  4. To send you the one email the account needs: the six-digit sign-up verification code, and the same code again if you ask us to resend it. There are no marketing emails.

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone for advertising, marketing or profiling. We do not build advertising profiles and do not share data with data brokers.

Who else processes your data

SYBL is a small app and relies on three service providers. They act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes.

Supabase — hosting, database, authentication and email

Your account and all of the data in the table above is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database in Ireland (Supabase’s West EU region). Supabase also handles sign-in and sends the verification email. Access is restricted at the database level so that one account can only ever read its own rows.

Anthropic — writing the wording of your tasks

Which areas you get today, at which difficulty, is decided entirely by our own server. A language model operated by Anthropic is then asked to write the wording for those slots. What is sent to Anthropic for that request is:

  • your chosen focus areas and their order of importance;
  • the four planned slots, each an area and a difficulty;
  • a summary of the tasks from your previous seven days, so today is not a repeat;
  • your current streak count.

Your name, email address, account identifier, password and time zone are not sent. The model is only ever asked to write text. It cannot award XP, change your level, alter a streak, or pick which areas you work on, and anything it returns that does not match the plan our server already made is discarded. If this step fails or is unavailable, your tasks are written from a built-in library instead and you will not notice the difference.

Apple and Google — app distribution

The app is distributed through the App Store and Google Play. When you download or update it, those stores process data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. They do not receive your SYBL data from us.

Where your data is stored, and for how long

Your data is held on servers operated by Supabase in Ireland (Supabase’s West EU region) and is transmitted between your phone and those servers over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Your login session is also stored on your own device so that you are not signed out every time you close the app; removing the app removes that copy.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. We do not have a separate archive and we do not retain a copy after deletion, other than in routine encrypted infrastructure backups, which rotate and expire on their own within a short period. There is no way to restore an account from those backups, including for you.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app. Open Profile from the top of the Today screen, scroll to Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap it.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your account, your email address, your display name and time zone, your onboarding answers, your focus areas, every task set and task, your XP, level, streaks and your whole XP history. It also ends your session on every device you were signed in on. Nothing is deactivated, archived or held back, and signing up again with the same email starts an entirely new account from zero.

If you cannot reach the in-app option — you have lost your phone, uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in — see the account deletion page for how to ask us to do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable format. If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, these rights come from the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR; if you are in California, from the CCPA/CPRA — and note that SYBL does not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

The fastest route for deletion is the in-app button described above. For anything else, write to adictbart@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or the UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to perform the contract you enter into by creating an account (providing the app itself), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

Children

SYBL is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, or 16 if you are in a country where that is the minimum age for consenting to online services. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, write to adictbart@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes. All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Database access is governed by row-level security rules, so an account can only ever read and write its own rows, and the values that matter — XP, levels, streaks — are calculated on the server and cannot be set by a modified app. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but if we ever discover a breach affecting your data we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
