Delete your account

You can erase your SYBL account and everything in it yourself. It takes about ten seconds and it is permanent.

Last updated September 3, 2026 · App: SYBL · Developer: SYBL

If you can still sign in

This is the fastest route, it needs nobody's help, and it is instant. In the SYBL app:

  1. Open the app and sign in

    You must be signed in to the account you want to delete.

  2. Tap Profile

    The link sits at the top of the Today screen.

  3. Scroll to “Delete account”

    It is the last section on the page.

  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm

    You will be shown exactly what is about to be destroyed and asked to type DELETE to confirm. That guard is there because there is no undo.

This cannot be reversed. Your level, your XP and every day you have recorded are gone the moment you confirm. Support cannot restore them, and signing up again with the same email address starts a completely new account from zero.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to the app — you uninstalled it, changed phones, or cannot get into your account — we will delete it for you.

  1. Email adictbart@gmail.com

    Send it from the email address the account was registered with. That address is how we confirm the account is yours, and we cannot act on a request sent from another address.

  2. Use the subject line “Delete my SYBL account”

    Nothing else is needed in the message.

  3. We delete it within 30 days

    In practice it is far sooner. We will reply to confirm once it is done. We will never ask you for your password.

What gets deleted

Everything. Deleting the account removes every one of these permanently:

  • your account and email address;
  • your password;
  • your display name and time zone;
  • your answers to the onboarding questions;
  • your chosen focus areas and their priority order;
  • every daily task set and every task in it, completed or not;
  • your XP total, level, current streak, longest streak, perfect days and tasks completed;
  • your entire XP history;
  • your sign-in sessions, on this device and on every other device the account was signed in on.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept in the live system. Your rows are removed immediately, not flagged as deleted or hidden from view, and there is no separate archive.

Copies may briefly survive in routine encrypted infrastructure backups taken before the deletion. Those backups rotate and expire on their own within a short period, they are never used to restore an individual account, and they are not accessible to anyone as your data. If we are ever required by law to retain a specific record, we will keep only that record and only for as long as the law requires.

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling SYBL removes the app and the sign-in session stored on your phone. It does not delete your account — your data stays on our servers and signing in again brings it all back. To erase the account you must use one of the two routes above.
