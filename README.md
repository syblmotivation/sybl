# SYBL

SYBL is a daily discipline app for iOS and Android. It sets four tasks a day
from the areas you choose to work on, and keeps score of whether you did them.

This site holds the two pages the app stores require to be publicly readable:

- **[Privacy Policy](https://syblmotivation.github.io/sybl/privacy/)** — what is
  collected, why, who else ever processes it, and how long it is kept.
- **[Delete your account](https://syblmotivation.github.io/sybl/delete-account/)**
  — how to erase your account and everything in it, from inside the app or by
  request.
