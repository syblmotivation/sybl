SYBL Support

Help with the SYBL app: getting in, getting your daily tasks, and getting out again if you want to.

Last updated September 22, 2026 · SYBL is currently available on iOS.

Contact

Write to adictbart@gmail.com.

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days. It helps if you say which screen you were on, what you expected, and what happened instead. Never send us your password.

Common questions

The verification email has not arrived

The code is six digits and usually arrives within a minute. Check the spam or junk folder, and check that the address you typed is the one you meant. On the verification screen you can request a new code once a minute. If nothing arrives after a few attempts, write to us with the address you used.

Resetting your password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email address, and we send a six-digit code. Enter that code together with your new password. The code expires after an hour; if it does, request another. Your old password keeps working until the new one is set.

The daily reminder

The reminder is off until you turn it on in Profile → Reminders, and it asks for notification permission only at that moment. It fires once a day, at the time you chose, and is cancelled for that day as soon as you complete a task. If nothing arrives, check that notifications for SYBL are allowed in the iOS Settings app; if you declined the permission, SYBL cannot ask again and it has to be granted there.

Signing in and your account

SYBL keeps you signed in between launches. If you are signed out unexpectedly, sign in again with the same email address — your tasks, XP, level and streak live on our servers, not on the phone, so nothing is lost by reinstalling the app. Your day rolls over at midnight in the time zone shown on Profile, which you can change there.

Deleting your account

You can erase your account and everything in it from Profile → Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, we will do it for you — the account deletion page explains both routes and lists exactly what is removed.

Reporting a bug

Send us the screen, what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, plus your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps. If you are testing SYBL through TestFlight, the feedback tool there reaches us with that detail attached.

Privacy

SYBL contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What it stores, why, and who else ever processes it is set out in the Privacy Policy.
